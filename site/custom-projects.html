Why this page exists

We set out to build three programs.
We ended up with a team that can build almost anything a school needs.

TomoClub's focus has been narrow on purpose: teach students social and emotional intelligence through game-based curriculum, teach them to create original ideas and then use AI to build on them without losing critical thinking, and educate educators to use AI well for themselves. Doing that properly meant hiring psychologists, education researchers, game designers, curriculum and instructional designers, and software engineers. That combination turns out to be rare, and school leaders started asking us for things that were not on our menu.

Program 1 · Grades 3–12

Social-emotional intelligence, taught through games

Multiplayer strategic gameplay with guided reflection. Students practise communication, collaboration, empathy and leadership because the game demands it, not because a worksheet asked.

  • 12 original multiplayer games, CASEL-aligned

Questions leaders ask

Before you fill in the form.

Is there a cost for the project outline?

No. The outline is free and yours to keep, whether or not you work with us. Budget ranges appear in the outline so there are no surprises later.

Do we have to use one of your existing programs?

No. If Future-Ready Skills, AI Literacy or AI PD fits the goal, we will say so because it's faster and cheaper for you. If not, we scope something new.

What if the problem has nothing to do with AI or students?

Bring it anyway. Staff workload, reporting, internal processes and communication are exactly the kinds of problems where a psychologist, an instructional designer and a software engineer in the same room produce something useful.

How do districts usually fund this?

Title II and Title IV, ESSER carry-over where it still exists, state workforce or career-readiness grants, foundation grants, and reallocation from PD or curriculum lines. The outline includes the funding routes we think apply, and we itemise curriculum and training separately because funders ask for it.

Who actually does the work?

The TomoClub team: psychologists, education researchers, curriculum and instructional designers, game designers and engineers, led by the co-founders. We don't hand projects to subcontractors.

How is student and staff data handled?

Our platform is GDPR and SOC 2 compliant. We sign your district's data privacy agreement and keep student data inside the scope you approve.
