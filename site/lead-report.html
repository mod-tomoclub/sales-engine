3. Fix these five things before trusting any number

  1. Exclude bots and the team. In GA4, Admin → Data collection → Data streams → Configure tag settings → Define internal traffic: add the office and home IP addresses as "internal", then in Admin → Data filters activate the Internal traffic filter. For the bot cities, create a saved comparison called "Real visitors" (Country = United States, City ≠ Boardman, City ≠ Ashburn) and apply it to every report you read.
  2. Tag every link you send. The 77% Direct figure means you cannot see which outreach works. Use this format on every email, LinkedIn post, newsletter and podcast note: 
    https://www.tomoclub.org/build-with-us/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=build-with-us-launch
https://www.tomoclub.org/build-with-us/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=custom-projects-sep26
https://www.tomoclub.org/build-with-us/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights
    The 22 drafted outreach emails should all carry the second link.
  3. Define key events. Admin → Events → mark lead_submitted as a key event, and create a new event calendly_click from cta_click where cta_href contains "calendly", then mark it too. From then on every channel and page shows a lead rate.
  4. Turn on Google signals and register the custom dimensions (section_name, engagement_ms, cta_text). Without the dimensions the section events are collected but invisible in reports.
  5. Retire the /contact-us iframe. Point the "Contact Us" buttons on the homepage at /build-with-us/ (or embed the same four-field form there). Forty-eight people a month reach that page and almost none stay.

5. Your weekly 30-minute review

Every Monday. Same order, same tabs, write three lines of notes at the end.

GA4 (10 min)

  • Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, comparison "Real visitors", last 7 days vs previous. Which source sent people to /build-with-us? Direct should be falling as UTMs spread.
  • Reports → Engagement → Pages and screens, filter page path contains "build-with-us". Views, users, average engagement time. Compare with /contact-us.
  • Reports → Engagement → Events → section_view, secondary dimension section_name. Read it top to bottom: hero → partners → how-it-works → programs → team → form. The biggest drop between two rows is next week's edit.
  • Key events: lead_submitted and calendly_click counts, and which source they came from.

Clarity (10 min)

  • Heatmaps → Scroll for /build-with-us: the line where 50% of visitors have left. Is the form above it?
  • Recordings, filter URL contains build-with-us, sort by "Rage clicks" and "Dead clicks". Watch three. Dead clicks on the program tiles mean people expect them to open something.
  • Dashboard → Insights: quick-backs (people who bounced within seconds) and JavaScript errors.
  • Filter: Clicked on "Send it over" to watch how real submitters behaved before submitting. Those are the sessions to learn from.

LinkedIn (5 min)

  • Measure → Signals manager: Insight Tag status. Must say Active.
  • Analyze / Measure → Website demographics, filter to the build-with-us URL, last 30 days: Job title, Job seniority, Company. You want Superintendent, Principal, Director of Curriculum near the top. If it is Teacher or Student, the traffic is coming from the wrong posts.
  • Plan → Audiences → Matched audience: once 300+ visitors have hit the page, create a "Build With Us visitors" audience for retargeting.

Leads sheet and Calendly (5 min)

  • New rows this week, and their Source column.
  • Every lead older than 24 hours has "Outline sent" filled in. If not, that is the only thing that matters this week.
  • Calendly bookings this week versus calendly_click in GA4: a big gap means people open Calendly and don't find a slot they like.

6. What to try, in order

  1. Send traffic before touching the page. The page cannot be optimised on 20 visits. The 22 personalised emails plus two founder LinkedIn posts a week, all with UTM links, should bring 100 to 150 school-leader visits in the first month. Everything below assumes that.
  2. Make the homepage send people here. The nav link exists; change the hero's "Contact Us" button and the closing CTA on the homepage to point at /build-with-us/. The homepage has 1,340 views a month and the form page has 55.
  3. Shorten the path to the form. The form is the sixth section. Test a compact version of the form directly under the hero (name, district, email, one line) with the full version left where it is. Compare form section_view rates and lead rate over two weeks.
  4. Use the section data to cut, not add. Whichever section has the steepest engagement drop in the section_view report gets shortened or moved below the form. Do one change per week so you can attribute the effect.
  5. Qualify the traffic, then retarget. When LinkedIn demographics show 100+ visitors with leadership titles, run a small retargeting campaign (US$10–15/day) to page visitors with the "initial project outline within 24 hours" promise. This is the "market to them again" step, and it needs no names.
  6. Follow-up sequence for leads. Every submission gets: outline within 24 h (already promised), a check-in at day 4, and a "still useful?" note at day 14. The Apps Script can push each row into HubSpot so this runs itself.
  7. Watch for "wrong audience" signals. If Clarity shows many mobile visits under 10 seconds from India, that is team or bot traffic and should be filtered, not "improved".
